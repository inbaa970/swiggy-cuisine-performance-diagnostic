# Swiggy Cuisine Performance Diagnostic - Data Story

## Overview

The Jan–Jun 2026 analysis shows clear differences in revenue performance across the six cuisines. Three cuisines exceeded their revenue targets, while three remained below target.

## Performance Breakdown

1. **North Indian — Above Target**
   Generated **₹1,98,681**, exceeding its **₹1,80,000** target by **₹18,681 (10.38%)**.

2. **South Indian — Above Target**
   Generated **₹67,616**, exceeding its **₹50,000** target.

3. **Italian — Above Target**
   Exceeded its assigned revenue target and is classified as **Above Target**.

4. **Chinese — Below Target - Watch**
   Remained below its target but within the **15% shortfall tolerance**.

5. **Fast Food — Below Target - Watch**
   Remained below its target and requires monitoring to improve performance.

6. **Desserts — Below Target - Critical**
   Generated revenue significantly below its **₹25,000** target and is classified as **Below Target - Critical**.

## Concrete Recommendations

1. **Focus improvement efforts on Desserts**
   Review the Desserts category performance and consider targeted promotions, bundles, or improved visibility to help close the significant gap against its revenue target.

2. **Monitor Chinese and Fast Food closely**
   These cuisines are below target but within the watch range. Review their revenue and order trends regularly and test targeted promotional actions to move them toward target achievement.
